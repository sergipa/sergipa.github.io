Spotimals Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Spotimals is a game made by TODO developer name ("we"). This page explains what information the game handles and why. The short version: the game has no accounts, it does not ask for your name, email or contacts, and the only personal data involved comes from the advertising network that shows ads in the game.

Your progress

Your level, coins and boosters are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: full-screen ads between levels and videos you can choose to watch for rewards. To serve and measure ads, Google's software collects device information such as the advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. Google's own policies apply to this data: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before loading ads, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy settings in the game. If you do not consent, ads may still be shown but they will not be personalised.

You can also remove the full-screen ads by buying the No Ads bundle in the game.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment details. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so that the game can give you what you bought and restore it later.

Usage data

To improve the game we record a small number of events about how it is played: which levels are started, won or lost and why, which boosters and offers are used, and whether a session ended normally. Each install is given a random identifier when the game is first opened; it is not linked to you, your device identifiers or your advertising identifier, and we cannot use it to identify you. These events are sent to PostHog (PostHog Inc., hosted in the European Union), which processes them on our behalf under its privacy policy. Your IP address is used to deliver the request and is not stored with the events.

You can switch this off at any time in Settings → Share usage data. Nothing is sent before you have answered the consent message where one is shown.

Children

Spotimals is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The ads are limited to content rated for general audiences.

Legal basis (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Personalised advertising: your consent. Usage data: our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the game, with the opt-out described above. Purchases: performance of the contract when you buy something.

Your rights

Because the game stores no account, there is no profile we could show, correct or delete beyond what is on your device; deleting the app removes it. For advertising data, use your device's advertising settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising; Android: Settings → Google → Ads) and the consent message in the game. For anything else, write to us at the address below and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
